# Owner profile — standing preferences and vetoes

## Standing vetoes

- Decomposition with no stopping rule, i.e. non-reproducible results. (20260726-1148)
- A claim asserted without cited evidence. (20260726-1148)
- Generated content stored indistinguishably from observed data. (20260726-1148)

## Preferences

- Wants agent reasoning recorded human-readably for feedback, not just structured payloads. (20260726-1148)
- Prefers incremental pilot promotion over batch application; accepts bounded residual risk on that basis. (20260726-1148)
- Answers design questions by rejecting wrong options rather than specifying right ones — present rival readings with consequences. (20260726-1148)
- Reframes the problem mid-interview when the framing is wrong; expect gate reopening rather than treating it as scope creep. (20260726-1148)

## Learned during implementation (20260726-1148)
- Two defects this session came from fusing DETECTION with POLICY in one function: the SSoT gate
  (measure vs escalate) and the soundness check (verify vs the thing being verified). Check for
  that fusion first when something is permanently red or suspiciously green.
- The owner accepts an honest recorded limit and rejects a pretended capability. NOT_EXPRESSIBLE
  is preferred to a forced verdict; UNCHECKED is preferred to a green tick that means nothing.
- Verify a criterion has teeth BEFORE building anything that depends on it. W0 cost almost
  nothing and would have invalidated the whole acceptance design had it failed.
